Privacy policy

This draft explains the categories of information the production service may need. It must be updated to match the final implementation and actual providers.

1. Information we may collect

Account details such as name and email, order and rental records, wallet transaction records, support messages, device and security logs, and information needed to provide a requested number service.

2. How information is used

To create and secure accounts, process orders, verify payments, request supplier fulfilment, provide support, prevent abuse and comply with legal obligations.

3. Service providers

The planned service uses Firebase for account and database services, Paystack for payment processing and a number supplier for inventory and fulfilment. Data shared with providers will be limited to what is necessary for the relevant service.

4. Security

Production systems should use server-side authorization, restricted database rules, secure secrets management and appropriate retention controls. No online service can guarantee absolute security.

5. Retention and your choices

Retention periods, access and deletion request procedures will be documented before launch and will depend on legal and operational requirements.

6. Cookies and analytics

Any analytics or cookie technologies will be disclosed here when selected and implemented.

7. Contact

Privacy contact details will be published before launch. This draft is not a substitute for a jurisdiction-specific privacy review.
