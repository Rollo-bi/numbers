How NumberPort works

A straightforward path from account creation to choosing a number and managing its activity.

01

Create an account

Register and manage your profile from a secure customer dashboard.

02

Choose a number

Browse by country, service and number type. Availability will be confirmed by the connected supplier.

03

Pay securely

Complete checkout through the hosted Paystack payment flow after payment integration is enabled.

04

Receive your number

After verified payment, the backend requests fulfilment from the supplier and records the result.

05

Manage activity

View orders, active rentals and incoming messages where supported by the selected product.

06

Get support

Contact support if an order needs attention or supplier fulfilment fails.

Payment and supplier fulfilment steps are planned workflows and are not active in this frontend preview.
