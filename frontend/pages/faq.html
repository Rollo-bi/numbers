Frequently asked questions

Quick answers about virtual numbers, rentals, payments and account use.

What is a temporary SMS number? ＋
A temporary SMS number is intended for a short activation workflow. Whether a specific platform accepts it depends on that platform and the number provider.
What is a long-term rental? ＋
A rental provides access to a number for a defined period. Features such as voice, SMS, renewal and porting vary by supplier and product.
Which countries will be supported? ＋
The initial target markets are the United States, United Kingdom, Canada, Germany and Australia. Live coverage will depend on verified supplier inventory.
How do payments work? ＋
The planned checkout uses Paystack. A backend service will verify successful payments before any order is fulfilled. Payment is not active in this preview.
Will my number be delivered automatically? ＋
Automatic delivery will be enabled after a supplier API is connected and tested. This frontend preview does not deliver real numbers.
Can I use the service for spam or fraud? ＋
No. Fraud, spam, impersonation, harassment and bypassing identity or security checks are prohibited.
